## Users

- Employers and hiring managers evaluating a hands-on technology engineer.
- Clients (businesses, hotels, property owners, SMEs) looking for someone to design, build, secure, and deploy real systems: networks, servers, software, smart-building systems.
- Potential business partners for the products Ademola builds (KiraOSINT, KiraCall, WellsTV, cloud platform).

They arrive from a link (CV, LinkedIn, GitHub, proposal) and decide within a minute whether Ademola is credible across the breadth claimed and worth contacting.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Ademola: present Ademola as a multidisciplinary engineer who designs, builds, integrates, troubleshoots, and deploys real-world systems across cybersecurity, infrastructure, cloud, software, and smart systems. Success = a visitor understands the breadth, sees concrete projects, and gets in touch.
